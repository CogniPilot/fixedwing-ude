Physics-Structured Temporal Neural Modeling of Commanded Fixed-Wing UAV Dynamics

Divyanshi Bansal · James Goppert — Purdue University

A 63 g Sport Cub S2 with a stabilising receiver, flown indoors under motion capture into and out of stall, is identified from pilot sticks in → aircraft motion out. Four models are compared on identical data. Everything on this page runs in your browser: the paper's physics is a Modelica model compiled and integrated by Rumoca (WebAssembly), and the neural residual runs beside it.

Temporal UDE

The physics below plus a bounded neural force/moment residual that also sees the last 1.05 s through a causal convolutional encoder.

Instantaneous UDE

The same residual, but a function of the current state and effective controls only.

ARX

A linear data-driven baseline on the nine outputs with 15 lagged stick samples.

OEM

Classical output-error fit of the 6-DOF physics: receiver map, input delays, motor torque, separation blend. No network.

Results of the reference run

Recursive forecasts on held-out windows. The score is the RMS error over φ, θ, p, q, r, V, each divided by a fixed scale (20°, 12°, 35°/s, 35°/s, 35°/s, 1 m/s); lower is better.

By flight regime

Every scored sample is labelled by the measured wing incidence relative to the stall prior: attached < 8.7°, transition 8.7–14.3°, separated ≥ 14.3°. RMSE in physical units.

The model, editable

This is the physics every forecast above integrates — the OEM, and the backbone of both UDEs. Pick whose identified parameters to load, edit anything, and forecast again. The network's residual ΔC and the separation state s_eff arrive as inputs each 1/60 s.

Data

Eight motion-capture records on a 60 Hz grid from three airframes of the same type (SC6, SC7, SC9). Whole manoeuvre arcs are assigned to fit, development or test; forecasts never straddle a role boundary.

Live forecasts are re-simulated in the browser and differ from the paper's stored forecasts by less than 3×10⁻³ (rad, rad/s, m/s): Rumoca integrates adaptively, the paper with RK4 at 1/120 s. Tables show the reference run.
